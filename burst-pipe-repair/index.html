A burst pipe is one of the most common water damage events a property can face, and potentially one of the most destructive. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

When a pipe bursts in Brooklyn Park or the surrounding area, Brooklyn Park Restoration runs an emergency response around the clock. We can help find the shutoff and stop the water, and our team works with licensed plumbers to see that the pipe itself is repaired correctly. Once the water source is stopped, we immediately begin extraction with truck-mounted and portable equipment, removing standing water from all affected areas as quickly as possible.

After the water is out, we dry every material that took on moisture: finished floors, subflooring, wood framing, insulation and drywall. Since water runs downhill through ceiling spaces, floor assemblies and wall cavities, a burst pipe frequently affects several rooms on more than one level. Our thermal imaging and moisture detection equipment traces water migration paths so every affected area is identified and properly dried, which prevents the mold growth and structural deterioration that hidden moisture causes.

Water travels through wall cavities, along electrical conduits, through floor assemblies and between ceiling joists, saturating materials in places you may not discover for days or weeks. Burst Pipe Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Insulate exposed pipes in unheated areas such as attics, garages and crawl spaces
  • Replace old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes before they fail unexpectedly
  • Open sink cabinets and leave a slow drip running on cold nights, which helps keep pipes from freezing
  • Pressure higher than 80 PSI wears on pipes and their fittings, so test yours from time to time
  • Put leak sensors by the water heater and along the main supply line
  • Find the main shutoff valve now and practice closing it so you can do it fast

Signs It Is Time for Burst Pipe Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Ceilings or walls that show damp patches, stains or a swollen bulge
  • A hidden leak often shows up first as a much larger water bill
  • A musty smell near a wall, cabinet or floor with plumbing behind it
  • Faucets that give only a trickle, or nothing, during a freeze
  • Tap water that turns brown, yellow or cloudy
  • A spray or steady drip coming off pipework you can see
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
